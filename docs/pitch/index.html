The problem

On 21 October, most people could not finish registering.

  • ✕ Time-outs
  • ✕ No ID
  • ✕ No screen reader support

Our answer

One script tag. One question per screen.

<script src="openpath.js"></script>
<openpath-flow questions="/questions/onboarding.json" api="https://api.example.org"></openpath-flow>

That is the whole install. The questions live in one JSON file.

Accessibility

Works with their tools. Brings its own.

The tools people already have

  • TalkBack, NVDA
  • Voice typing
  • Keyboard, zoom to 400%

Ours, for those who have none

  • Read aloud
  • Speak your answer
  • Simple words
  • Text size, contrast

Every helper is off until the learner turns it on, so OpenPath never talks over a screen reader.
